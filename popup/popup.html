InstaBrake

This extension tries to give the power back to the users to use social media for what it was created for: see and follow who you want without falling into an endless black hole of meaningless content.

Privacy and conditions

InstaBrake will never track you. It’s free. It only needs ‘storage’ permissions to save your options.
